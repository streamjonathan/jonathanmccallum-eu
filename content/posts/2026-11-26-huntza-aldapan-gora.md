In the video, I am in a green sweater with headphones, reacting to the song.
Bideoan, jertse berde batekin eta entzungailuekin nago, abestiari erantzuten.

The song is "Aldapan Gora" by Huntza.
"Aldapan Gora" abestia da, Huntzarena.

Huntza is a Basque folk rock band from Bilbao.
Huntza Bilboko folk rock talde euskalduna da.

The band formed in 2014.
Taldea 2014an sortu zen.

The word Huntza means "ivy" in Basque.
Huntza hitzak "ivy" esan nahi du ingelesez.

"Aldapan Gora" means "Up the Hill".
"Aldapan Gora"-k "Up the Hill" esan nahi du.

The single came out in November 2016.
Singlea 2016ko azaroan atera zen.

Its music video became the most viewed song in Basque on YouTube.
Bere bideoklipa euskarazko abestirik ikusiena bihurtu zen YouTuben.

The song was included in their first studio album, "Ertzetatik" (2016).
Abestia haien lehen estudio-albumean sartu zen, "Ertzetatik" (2016).

The video clip was filmed in Mutriku, a seaside town full of hills.
Bideoklipa Mutrikun filmatu zen, muinoz betetako itsasertzeko herri batean.

You can hear the sound of the trikitixa, the Basque accordion, in the song.
Abestian trikitixaren soinua entzun dezakezu, euskal akordeoiarena.

I like this song because it is about wandering, aimlessly, through streets and mountains.
Abesti hau gustatzen zait kaleetan eta mendietan zehar helbururik gabe ibiltzeari buruzkoa delako.

I know that feeling.
Sentimendu hori ezagutzen dut.

I get lost in the streets, and I get lost in the mountains too.
Kaleetan galtzen naiz, eta mendietan ere galtzen naiz.

But the song is happy, and the music keeps moving up the hill.
Baina abestia alaia da, eta musika muinoan gora mugitzen jarraitzen du.

I like hearing the same song in two ways.
Abesti bera bi modutan entzutea gustatzen zait.

The rock version is full of energy.
Rock bertsioa energiaz beteta dago.

The orchestra version is big and warm.
Orkestra bertsioa handia eta beroa da.

Both of them make me want to walk up a hill in Mutriku.
Biek Mutrikun muino bat igotzeko gogoa ematen didate.

Have you heard this song?
Entzun duzu abesti hau?

Which version do you like more, the rock version or the orchestra version?
Zein bertsio gustatzen zaizu gehiago, rock bertsioa ala orkestra bertsioa?
