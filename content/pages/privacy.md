# Privacy — Pribatutasuna

This site is run by Jonathan McCallum, who lives in Getaria, in the Basque Country. It is a small personal site: writing, and nothing else. No accounts, no tracking, no adverts, nothing for sale. It follows European privacy law (GDPR) for visitors everywhere.

Gune hau Jonathan McCallumek kudeatzen du, Getarian bizi dena, Euskal Herrian. Gune pertsonal txiki bat da: idazlanak, eta besterik ez. Ez konturik, ez jarraipenik, ez iragarkirik, ez ezer salgai. Europako pribatutasun-legea (DBEO) jarraitzen du, bisitari guztientzat, edonon daudela ere.

If you have a question about this page or your own personal data, send Jonathan <a href="#" data-contact-email data-contact-email-text>an email</a>. You can also message him on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/jonamccallum) or [Threads](https://www.threads.com/@jonamccallum).

Orrialde honi edo zure datu pertsonalei buruzko galderaren bat baduzu, bidali Jonathan-i <a href="#" data-contact-email data-contact-email-text>mezu bat</a>. [Instagramen](https://www.instagram.com/jonamccallum) edo [Threadsen](https://www.threads.com/@jonamccallum) ere idatz diezaiokezu.

## Who is responsible for your data — Zure datuen arduraduna nor da

Jonathan McCallum is the data controller for this site. That means he decides what is collected and why. He is not a company, and he has no data protection officer. A small personal site like this does not need one.

Jonathan McCallum da gune honen datu-arduraduna. Horrek esan nahi du berak erabakitzen duela zer biltzen den eta zertarako. Ez da enpresa bat, eta ez du datu-babeseko arduradunik. Gune pertsonal txiki honek ez du horren beharrik.

## What is collected, and why — Zer biltzen da, eta zertarako

### The theme choice — Gaiaren aukera

The site remembers only whether you chose the light or dark theme, on your own device. Nothing else is stored. If you refuse cookies, nothing is stored at all.

Guneak gogoratzen du bakarrik gai argia edo iluna aukeratu zenuen, zure gailuan. Ez da besterik gordetzen. Cookieak ukatzen badituzu, ez da ezer gordetzen.

### Search — Bilaketa

Search runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Bilaketa zure nabigatzailean egiten da erabat. Idazten duzuna ez da inora bidaltzen.

### Contact — Harremana

If you email Jonathan, he reads your message and replies. Your email address is used only to reply to you. Nothing is shared with anyone else.

Jonathan-i mezu bat bidaltzen badiozu, berak irakurri eta erantzuten du. Zure helbide elektronikoa erantzuna emateko bakarrik erabiltzen da. Ez da inorekin partekatzen.

## Your rights — Zure eskubideak

Under GDPR you can ask for a copy of your data, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to how it is used. Just email Jonathan — see above.

DBEOraren arabera, zure datuen kopia bat eska dezakezu, zuzentzeko edo ezabatzeko eskatu, edo nola erabiltzen diren aurka egin. Bidali Jonathan-i mezu bat — ikusi goian.
